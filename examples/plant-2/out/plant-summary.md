## Documents

- **MP-501 Filler Lubrication and Valve Seal Inspection** (Rev B, effective 2020-02-17, owner Maintenance). Keep the rotary fillers filling accurately by lubricating the carousel and catching worn valve seals.
- **MP-502 Case Packer Vacuum Pump Inspection** (Rev A, effective 2019-08-12, owner Maintenance). Find a failing vacuum pump before it stops the case packer.
- **QP-303 Fill Weight Checks** (Rev F, effective 2024-01-15, owner Quality). Keep every bottle at or above the label claim without giving product away.
- **QP-306 First-Off Approval** (Rev C, effective 2024-05-20, owner Quality). Confirm the first product after a start-up or changeover is right before the line runs at rate.
- **QP-308 Incoming Inspection of Packaging Materials** (Rev D, effective 2023-10-09, owner Quality). Stop bad bottles, caps, labels and cases at the dock, before they reach a line.
- **QP-309 Packaging Material Specifications and Approved Suppliers** (Rev G, effective 2025-01-13, owner Quality). Hold in one place the specification and the approved supplier for each packaging material.
- **QP-315 Nonconforming Product and Hold** (Rev C, effective 2022-04-18, owner Quality). Keep product or material that might be wrong from being shipped or used.
- **QP-316 Customer Complaints and Lot Trace** (Rev B, effective 2020-11-02, owner Quality). Answer every customer complaint and find all product from the same lot.
- **SOP-101 Line Start-Up** (Rev E, effective 2025-06-16, owner Production). Start a packaging line safely and reach approved production in the shortest time.
- **SOP-102 Line Shutdown and Shift Handover** (Rev C, effective 2024-09-02, owner Production). Leave the line clean, safe and documented for the next shift.
- **SOP-104 Size Changeover, Line 2 (2 L and 500 ml)** (Rev B, effective 2021-03-15, owner Production). Change Line 2 between the 2 L and the 500 ml fabric softener bottle.
- **SOP-105 Product Changeover and Line Clearance, Lines 1 and 3** (Rev B, effective 2021-02-08, owner Production). Change between products of the same bottle size without mixing product, labels or caps.
- **SP-402 Lockout/Tagout** (Rev D, effective 2025-03-03, owner Safety). Make sure no machine can start or release stored energy while someone is working inside it.
- **SP-405 Forklift and Pedestrian Safety** (Rev C, effective 2024-08-05, owner Safety). Keep forklifts and people apart in the warehouse and at the dock doors.
- **TR-701 Training and Qualification** (Rev D, effective 2024-02-12, owner Human Resources). Make sure nobody carries out a procedure they have not been trained and signed off on.
- **WH-601 Receiving and Put-Away** (Rev C, effective 2023-06-19, owner Warehouse). Receive materials safely, check them against the order, and keep them in quarantine until Quality releases them.
- **WI-210 Filler Setup and Nozzle Check** (Rev D, effective 2023-04-03, owner Production). Set up a rotary filler so that every nozzle fills inside the weight limits.
- **WI-211 Capper Setup and Torque Check** (Rev C, effective 2022-11-07, owner Production). Apply caps at the right torque so bottles neither leak nor are too hard to open.
- **WI-212 Labeler Setup and Label Roll Change** (Rev D, effective 2025-09-08, owner Production). Apply labels straight, wrinkle-free and in the right place, and change rolls without a line stop.
- **WI-213 Case Packer Setup and Glue Check** (Rev B, effective 2022-08-22, owner Production). Erect, pack and seal shipper cases that survive the pallet.

## Rules

- **documents to update after supplier approval** (QP-309, step 3): After approval the Quality Manager updates QP-309, QP-308 and the label and cap setup instructions before the material is used.
- **inspection of critical characteristics** (QP-309, step 4): Characteristics marked critical must be measured on every lot by the QA Technician; label liner caliper is critical.
- **lockout method for case packers** (MP-502, 7.0 step 1): Case packers are locked out using the group lock box.
- **lockout method for case packers** (SP-402, step 5 / Revision history): One person, one lock, one key: each person applies their own lock to the hasp. The group lock box was removed in Rev D.
- **lockout method for case packers** (WI-213, step 7): For any work inside the guards the Operator locks out as described in SP-402 (no revision given).
- **lockout scope** (SP-402, Scope): Lockout applies to all servicing, cleaning and jam clearing that puts any part of the body inside a guard, on any equipment at Plant 2.
- **lockout scope for Line 2 size changeover** (SOP-104, 6.0 step 4): Only FIL-2 and CAP-2 are locked out, though changes are also made on LAB-2, CPK-2 and CNV-2.
- **maximum forklift speed** (SP-405, step 2): Forklifts may not exceed 5 mph anywhere inside the building.
- **maximum forklift speed** (WH-601, step 3): On the dock and in the receiving lanes forklifts drive no faster than 3 mph.
- **which suppliers' packaging may be used** (QP-308, step 2): The QA Technician rejects a lot whose supplier is not on the approved list in QP-309.
- **which suppliers' packaging may be used** (QP-309, step 1): Purchasing buys only from the approved supplier in the table.
- **which suppliers' packaging may be used** (WI-212, step 1): Rolls from a supplier not listed in QP-309 are not to be loaded.
- **who applies lockout locks** (SP-402, Roles): Authorized Employees (trained and listed Operators and Maintenance Technicians) apply their own lock and tag.
- **who approves scrap above 500 dollars** (QP-315, step 6): Scrap above 500 dollars needs the Quality Manager's signature.
- **who approves suppliers** (QP-309, Roles / step 2): The Quality Manager owns the document and approves suppliers; a new or second supplier needs a line trial and a signed FRM-QA-09 before first production order.
- **who checks the dock before entering a trailer** (SP-405, step 7): The Forklift Driver confirms wheels are chocked and the dock leveler is locked before entering a trailer.
- **who checks the dock before entering a trailer** (WH-601, step 2): The Forklift Driver chocks the trailer wheels and locks the dock leveler before entering.
- **who flushes the supply line** (SOP-105, 6.0 step 4): The Batching Operator flushes the supply line from MIX-1 or MIX-2.
- **who gives first-off approval** (QP-306, step 7): The QA Technician checks and signs first-off approval.
- **who gives first-off approval** (SOP-101, step 13): The QA Technician approves or rejects the first-off as described in QP-306.
- **who gives first-off approval** (SOP-105, 7.0 step 3): The QA Technician gives first-off approval, including a label and lot code check.
- **who holds the line until first-off approval** (QP-306, step 8): The Line Lead holds the line until approval and corrects any fault.
- **who may change the fill setpoint** (QP-303, step 6): Operators must not change the fill setpoint.
- **who may change the fill setpoint** (QP-303, step 7): Only the Maintenance Technician changes the setpoint and the change is written on the chart.
- **who may change the fill setpoint** (WI-210, step 5): The Operator may adjust the fill setpoint by up to 2 g up or down and writes the change on FRM-PR-10.
- **who may change the fill setpoint** (WI-210, step 6): If more than 2 g of adjustment is needed in one shift the Operator stops and calls the Maintenance Technician.
- **who moves held product to the hold cage** (QP-315, step 2): The Team Leader moves held product to the hold cage the same shift.
- **who moves held product to the hold cage** (SOP-102, step 4): The Operator moves any product on hold to the hold cage at shutdown.
- **who places product on hold** (QP-303, Roles): The QA Technician places product on hold.
- **who places product on hold** (QP-303, step 5): The Line Lead places all product since the last good check on hold.
- **who places product on hold** (QP-315, Roles): Anyone may place product on hold.
- **who places product on hold** (QP-316, 7.0 step 3): The QA Technician places any remaining stock from the same lot on hold.
- **who places product on hold** (WI-211, step 7): The Operator puts product capped since the last good check on hold.
- **who places product on hold** (WI-212, step 10): The Operator puts a roll aside with a hold tag after three jams in an hour.
- **who releases packaging materials** (QP-308, Roles / step 8): The QA Technician releases or rejects each lot.
- **who releases packaging materials** (WH-601, step 6): Packaging materials stay in the quarantine lane until the QA Technician releases them.
- **who releases packaging materials** (WI-212, step 1): Operators load only label rolls with a green QA release sticker.
- **who releases the line** (MP-501, 3.0 Responsibilities): The Shift Lead releases the line.
- **who releases the line** (SOP-101, Roles): The Line Lead owns the start-up and signs the checklist.
- **who releases the line** (SOP-104, 3.0 Responsibilities): The Shift Lead releases the line for changeover and signs the record.
- **who removes lockout locks** (MP-501, 6.0 step 4): The Maintenance Technician removes the locks after the lubrication work.
- **who removes lockout locks** (SOP-101, Roles / step 2): The Maintenance Technician removes locks after maintenance work, and the Line Lead confirms all locks and tags are removed.
- **who removes lockout locks** (SOP-104, 7.0 step 7): The Maintenance Technician removes locks and tags after the changeover.
- **who removes lockout locks** (SP-402, step 8): Each person removes only their own lock; a lock left at shift change stays until its owner removes it or the Safety Manager authorises removal.
- **who repeats the nozzle check after a seal change** (MP-501, 7.0 step 4): After any seal change the operator, not the Maintenance Technician, repeats the WI-210 nozzle check before the line is released.
- **who services the vacuum pump** (WI-213, step 6): Operators check the cups, then call the Maintenance Technician for low vacuum.
- **who sets application torque** (WI-211, step 3): The Operator sets the application torque from the table.
- **who signs off new operators** (TR-701, step 2): The Line Lead signs off a new operator on FRM-TR-02 for each required procedure.
- **who supplies shift records for a lot trace** (QP-316, 7.0 step 4): The Shift Lead gives the QA Technician the shift's records.
- **who tells the Training Coordinator of a revision** (TR-701, step 3): The document owner tells the Training Coordinator on the day of issue.
- **who verifies line clearance** (SOP-105, 6.0 step 5): The Shift Lead walks the line with FRM-PR-05 and signs only when nothing from the old product remains.

## Known conflicts in the documents

Do not answer a question that touches one of these without saying the documents disagree.

- Label liner caliper is critical on every lot (QP-309 Rev G) but QP-308 does not check it. QP-309 Rev G made label liner caliper (0.075 mm plus or minus 0.005) a critical characteristic that must be measured on every lot. QP-308 Rev D (2023, older than QP-309 Rev G) lists the label checks at incoming inspection and liner caliper is not among them. QP-309 step 3 also requires QP-308 to be updated after a change.
- QP-315 points to QP-318, which is not in the set. The document is referenced but was not among the files provided. It may be missing, withdrawn or renumbered.
- WI-212 points to WI-215, which is not in the set. The document is referenced but was not among the files provided. It may be missing, withdrawn or renumbered.
- MP-502 cites SP-402 Rev B; the current revision is D. Work may be following a withdrawn version.
- TR-701 cites WI-211 Rev A; the current revision is C. Work may be following a withdrawn version.
- SOP-101, SOP-104 and MP-501 have the Maintenance Technician remove locks; SP-402 says each person removes only their own. SP-402 step 8 lets each person remove only their own lock, and Operators can be Authorized Employees. SOP-101, SOP-104 and MP-501 assign lock removal to the Maintenance Technician alone, which cannot cover a lock an operator applied.
- MP-502 still tells technicians to use the group lock box that SP-402 Rev D removed. MP-502 step 7.1 locks out the case packer with the group lock box per SP-402 Rev B. SP-402 Rev D says 'Group lock box removed' and requires one person, one lock, one key. WI-213 points to SP-402 without a revision, so only MP-502 is out of date.
- Two answers to who may change the fill setpoint. WI-210 Rev D (2023) lets operators adjust the setpoint by up to 2 g. QP-303 Rev F (2024) says operators must not change it and only the Maintenance Technician may. WI-210 was not revised after QP-303, and TR-701 trains operators on WI-210.
- Two different roles move held product to the hold cage. QP-315 step 2 makes the Team Leader responsible for moving held product to the hold cage the same shift. SOP-102 step 4 has the Operator do it at shutdown. Team Leader is not defined anywhere else.
- Forklift speed limit: 5 mph everywhere (SP-405) versus 3 mph on the dock (WH-601). SP-405 step 2 says the maximum is 5 mph everywhere inside the building, including dock doors. WH-601 step 3 says no faster than 3 mph on the dock and in the receiving lanes. A driver who reads only SP-405 would think 5 mph is allowed at the dock.
- Two values for fill weight check interval (Lines 1, 2 and 3). QP-303 says 1 hour; SOP-101 says 30 minutes
- Two torque values for the same 38 mm cap. WI-211 Rev C raised the 38 mm cap torque from 15 to 17 in-lb after leak complaints. SOP-104 step 7.3 still tells the Operator to set 15 in-lb, and TR-701 requires operators to be trained on WI-211 Rev A, the version before the change. Line 2 operators following SOP-104 or trained to TR-701 would set the old torque.
