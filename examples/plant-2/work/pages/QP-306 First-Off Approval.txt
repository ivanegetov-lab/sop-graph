PURPOSE

Confirm the first product after a start-up or changeover is right before the line runs at rate.

SCOPE

Every start-up, product changeover and size changeover on Lines 1, 2 and 3.

ROLES

 ROLE                                    RESPONSIBILITY


 QA Technician                           Carries out the checks and signs.

 Operator                                Supplies the samples.

 Line Lead                               Holds the line until approval.


PPE
     Safety glasses
     Safety shoes

TOOLS

     Check scale (0.1 g)
     Torque tester
     Approved label artwork binder

M AT E R I A L S

None.

PROCEDURE

  Checks

 STEP         WHO                         ACTION

 1            Operator                    Bring the first 5 good bottles and one packed case to the QA bench.

 2            QA Technician               Fill weight: all 5 bottles inside the limits in WI-210.

 3            QA Technician               Cap: correct cap, removal torque inside the range in WI-211.

 4            QA Technician               Label: correct artwork against the binder, straight within 2 mm, no
                                          wrinkles or bubbles.
 5            QA Technician                       Lot code: readable, correct format (see QP-316), correct date and shift.

 6            QA Technician                       Case: correct count, case label matches the product, glue seal good.


  Decision

 STEP         WHO                                 ACTION


 7            QA Technician                       All checks pass: sign FRM-QA-06 First-Off Record and tell the Line Lead.

 8            QA Technician                       Any check fails: the line stays stopped. The Line Lead corrects the fault
                                                  and a new first-off is taken.

 9            Line Lead                           Do not run above 50% speed before approval.
