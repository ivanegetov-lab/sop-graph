                                                                                                         SOP-102
     Line Shutdown and Shift Handover                                                                             Rev C


  Effective                        Owner                           Prepared by                 Approved by
  2024-09-02                       Production                      M. Alvarez                  D. Whitfield


PURPOSE

Leave the line clean, safe and documented for the next shift.

SCOPE

Lines 1, 2 and 3, at the end of every shift.

ROLES

 ROLE                    RESPONSIBILITY


 Line Lead               Writes the handover and meets the incoming Line Lead.

 Operator                Cleans and secures the machine.


PPE

     Safety glasses
     Safety shoes
     Chemical-resistant gloves when cleaning the filler

TOOLS
     Scraper
     Lint-free wipes

M AT E R I A L S

     Sanitizer

PROCEDURE

  Shutdown

 STEP         WHO                            ACTION

 1            Operator                       Run the line empty. Stop the filler, then the capper, labeler and case
                                             packer in that order.

 2            Operator                       Clean filler nozzles and the drip tray. Wipe the labeler rollers. Empty the
                                             case packer reject bin.

 3            Operator                       Count rejected bottles and write the count on the production report.

 4            Operator                       Move any product on hold to the hold cage as described in QP-315.
  Handover

 STEP         WHO                               ACTION

 5            Operator                          Enter every stop of 3 minutes or longer in FRM-PR-09 Downtime Log
                                                Sheet with the cause and a short comment.

 6            Line Lead                         Fill in FRM-PR-02 Shift Handover Log: output against plan, open
                                                problems, materials short, safety concerns.

 7            Line Lead                         Walk the line with the incoming Line Lead. Both sign the log.

 8            Line Lead                         If the line will be down for more than 60 minutes, the next start follows
                                                SOP-101 in full.


R E L AT E D D O C U M E N T S

     SOP-101 Line Start-Up
     QP-315 Nonconforming Product and Hold

RECORDS

     FRM-PR-02 Shift Handover Log
     FRM-PR-09 Downtime Log Sheet
