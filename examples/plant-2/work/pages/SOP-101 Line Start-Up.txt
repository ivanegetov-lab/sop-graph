  Effective                        Owner                           Prepared by                  Approved by
  2025-06-16                       Production                      M. Alvarez                   D. Whitfield


PURPOSE

Start a packaging line safely and reach approved production in the shortest time.

SCOPE

Lines 1, 2 and 3. Applies at the start of every shift and after any stop longer than 60 minutes.

ROLES

 ROLE                                              RESPONSIBILITY


 Line Lead                                         Owns the start-up and signs the checklist.

 Operator                                          Carries out the machine checks.

 QA Technician                                     Gives first-off approval.

 Maintenance Technician                            Removes locks after maintenance work.


PPE

     Safety glasses
     Safety shoes
     Hearing protection in the filling hall

TOOLS

None.

M AT E R I A L S
     Bottles
     Caps
     Labels
     Shipper cases
     Hot-melt glue

PROCEDURE

  Before start

 STEP          WHO                            ACTION

 1             Line Lead                      Read the previous shift's entry in FRM-PR-02 Shift Handover Log. Note
                                              any open problems.
 2          Line Lead                Confirm with the Maintenance Technician that all locks and tags are
                                     removed as described in SP-402.

 3          Operator                 Walk the line from filler to palletizer. Remove tools, rags and loose parts.
                                     All guards closed.

 4          Operator                 Check compressed air at the line regulator: 6.2 bar minimum.

 5          Operator                 Confirm the correct bottles, caps, labels and cases are staged for the
                                     scheduled product.


 Machine checks

 STEP       WHO                      ACTION

 6          Operator                 Filler (FIL-1, FIL-2, FIL-3): set up as described in WI-210.

 7          Operator                 Capper (CAP-1, CAP-2, CAP-3): set up as described in WI-211.

 8          Operator                 Labeler (LAB-1, LAB-2, LAB-3): set up as described in WI-212.

 9          Operator                 Case packer (CPK-1, CPK-2, CPK-3): set up as described in WI-213.
                                     Glue tank at temperature before starting.

 10         Operator                 Palletizer (PAL-1, PAL-2, PAL-3): light curtain test, correct pallet pattern
                                     selected.


 Start and first-off

 STEP       WHO                      ACTION

 11         Operator                 Start conveyors (CNV-1, CNV-2, CNV-3), then the filler at 50% speed.

 12         Operator                 Run 20 bottles and stop. Take the first-off samples to the QA Technician.

 13         QA Technician            Approve or reject the first-off as described in QP-306.

 14         Operator                 After approval, ramp to the scheduled rate over 5 minutes.

 15         Operator                 Take the first fill weight check within 15 minutes of start, then every 30
                                     minutes for the rest of the shift, as described in QP-303.

 16         Line Lead                Sign FRM-PR-01 Start-Up Checklist and file it at the line.
