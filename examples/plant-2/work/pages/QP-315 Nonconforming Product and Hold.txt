 PLANT 2                   Dept: Quality               Effective: 2022-04-18
                                                                                                Appr: H. Costa


 Purpose

Keep product or material that might be wrong from being shipped or used.

 Scope

Finished product, product in process and packaging materials.

 Roles

 Role                            Responsibility

 Anyone                          May place product on hold.

 QA Technician                   Decides what happens to held product.

 Team Leader                     Makes sure held product is tagged and moved.

 Quality Manager                 Approves scrap above 500 dollars.


 PPE                                       Tools                                 Materials

     Safety shoes                           Red HOLD tags                      None.

 Procedure

Placing a hold
 Step      Who                      Action

 1         Anyone                   Attach FRM-QA-15 Hold Tag to every pallet, roll or tote in question. Write the
                                    reason, the lot code and your name.

 2         Team Leader              Move held product to the hold cage the same shift. Nothing on hold stays at
                                    the line.

 3         Team Leader              Tell the QA Technician.

Disposition
 Step      Who                      Action

 4         QA Technician            Inspect within one working day. Decide: release, rework, return to supplier or
                                    scrap.

 5         QA Technician            Rework follows QP-318.

 6         QA Technician            Record the decision on FRM-QA-16 Disposition Record. Scrap above 500
                                    dollars needs the Quality Manager's signature.
 7          QA Technician                   If held product has already shipped, start a lot trace as described in QP-316.
