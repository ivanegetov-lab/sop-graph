     PLANT 2 · QUALITY PROCEDURE
                                                                                                           QP-303
     Fill Weight Checks                                                                                          Rev F


  Effective                    Owner                           Prepared by                   Approved by
  2024-01-15                   Quality                         P. Nakamura                   H. Costa


PURPOSE

Keep every bottle at or above the label claim without giving product away.

SCOPE

All filled product on Lines 1, 2 and 3.

ROLES

 ROLE                                  RESPONSIBILITY


 Operator                              Takes and weighs the samples.

 Line Lead                             Reacts to results outside the action limits.

 QA Technician                         Reviews the charts each shift and places product on hold.

 Maintenance Technician                Adjusts the filler.


PPE

     Safety glasses
     Safety shoes

TOOLS

     Check scale (0.1 g)
     Tare bottles

M AT E R I A L S
None.

PROCEDURE

  Sampling

 STEP         WHO                         ACTION


 1            Operator                    Once every hour, take 5 bottles in a row from the filler discharge.

 2            Operator                    Weigh each bottle on the check scale. Subtract the tare. Write the five
                                          weights and their average on FRM-QA-03 Fill Weight Chart.

 3            Operator                    Verify the check scale with the 1 kg test weight at the start of every shift.
  Reacting

 STEP         WHO                                 ACTION

 4            Operator                            Any single bottle below the lower limit: stop the line and tell the Line
                                                  Lead.

 5            Line Lead                           Place all product since the last good check on hold as described in QP-
                                                  315. Call the QA Technician.

 6            Operator                            Average above target by more than 4 g for two checks in a row: tell the
                                                  Line Lead. Operators must not change the fill setpoint.

 7            Line Lead                           Call the Maintenance Technician to adjust the filler. Only the Maintenance
                                                  Technician changes the setpoint, and the change is written on the chart.

 8            QA Technician                       Review every chart before the end of the shift and initial it.

Note: Limits and targets for each product are in WI-210.

R E L AT E D D O C U M E N T S

     WI-210 Filler Setup and Nozzle Check
     QP-315 Nonconforming Product and Hold

RECORDS

     FRM-QA-03 Fill Weight Chart
