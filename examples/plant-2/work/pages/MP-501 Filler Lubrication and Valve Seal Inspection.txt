        PLANT 2                        Filler Lubrication and Valve Seal Inspection
   Maintenance Procedure
                              Document No.: MP-501                 Revision: B        Effective: 2020-02-17

 Department: Maintenance      Prepared by: K. Petrov               Approved by: J. Brandt


1.0 PURPOSE
Keep the rotary fillers filling accurately by lubricating the carousel and catching worn valve seals.

2.0 SCOPE
FIL-1, FIL-2 and FIL-3. Covers the 14-day lubrication task and the 90-day valve seal inspection in the
maintenance system.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

 Role                                         Responsibility

 Maintenance Technician                       Carries out the work.

 Maintenance Planner                          Schedules the work with production.

 Shift Lead                                   Releases the line.


4.0 SAFETY AND MATERIALS
Personal protective equipment: Safety glasses; Nitrile gloves; Safety shoes
Materials: Food-grade grease (H1); Fill valve seal kit
Tools: Grease gun; Nozzle wrench; Seal pick; Inspection light

5.0 RELATED DOCUMENTS AND RECORDS
Documents: SP-402 Lockout/Tagout; WI-210 Filler Setup and Nozzle Check
Records: Maintenance work order

6.0 EVERY 14 DAYS: LUBRICATION AND NOZZLE INSPECTION
 1. Maintenance Technician: Lock out the filler as described in SP-402.
 2. Maintenance Technician: Grease the carousel bearing and the lift cams: 3 strokes at each point. Use
    only food-grade H1 grease.
 3. Maintenance Technician: Inspect every nozzle tip for nicks and build-up. Clean or replace.
 4. Maintenance Technician: Remove locks. Run the filler empty for 2 minutes and listen for noise.

7.0 EVERY 90 DAYS: VALVE SEAL INSPECTION
 1. Maintenance Technician: Lock out. Remove two valves, chosen from different sides of the carousel.
 2. Maintenance Technician: Inspect the seals for flat spots, cuts and swelling. If either valve shows
    wear, inspect all valves.
 3. Maintenance Technician: Replace worn seals. If more than a third are worn, replace the full set.
 4. Maintenance Technician: After any seal change, the operator repeats the nozzle check in WI-210
    before the line is released.
 5. Maintenance Technician: Write what was found, valve by valve, on the work order. Do not close it
    with 'OK' alone.
