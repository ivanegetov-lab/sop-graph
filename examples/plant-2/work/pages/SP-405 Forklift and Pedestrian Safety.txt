PURPOSE

Keep forklifts and people apart in the warehouse and at the dock doors.

SCOPE

Forklifts FL-01 to FL-04, the warehouse, and dock doors 1 to 4.

ROLES

 ROLE                                    RESPONSIBILITY


 Forklift Driver                         Holds a current licence and does the daily check.

 Warehouse Supervisor                    Keeps walkways and sightlines clear.

 Safety Manager                          Reviews near-miss reports every week.


PPE
     High-visibility vest
     Safety shoes

TOOLS

None.

M AT E R I A L S

None.

PROCEDURE

  Driving

 STEP         WHO                        ACTION

 1            Forklift Driver            Complete FRM-SF-05 Forklift Daily Check before first use each shift. A
                                         truck with a fault is tagged out.

 2            Forklift Driver            Maximum speed is 5 mph everywhere inside the building.

 3            Forklift Driver            Sound the horn at every corner, doorway and dock door. Stop if a
                                         pedestrian is within 3 metres.

 4            Forklift Driver            Travel with forks lowered. Reverse if the load blocks the view.
  Pedestrians and docks

 STEP         WHO                                 ACTION

 5            Anyone                              Walk only in the marked green walkways. Make eye contact with the
                                                  driver before crossing.

 6            Warehouse Supervisor                Nothing is stacked within 2 metres of a corner or a dock door where it
                                                  could block a driver's view.

 7            Forklift Driver                     Before entering a trailer: wheels chocked, dock leveler (DOCK-1 to
                                                  DOCK-4) locked in place.

 8            Anyone                              Report every near miss on FRM-SF-06 Near-Miss Card, the same shift.
