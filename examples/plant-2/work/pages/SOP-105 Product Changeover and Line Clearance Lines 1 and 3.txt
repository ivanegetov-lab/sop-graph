        PLANT 2                Product Changeover and Line Clearance, Lines 1 and 3
     Standard Operating
         Procedure           Document No.: SOP-105               Revision: B       Effective: 2021-02-08

 Department: Production      Prepared by: R. Okafor              Approved by: J. Brandt


1.0 PURPOSE
Change between products of the same bottle size without mixing product, labels or caps.

2.0 SCOPE
Line 1 (AC-1000, AC-1000-LEM) and Line 3 (DS-0750, DS-0750-ECO). The clearance steps also apply
to Line 2 size changeovers.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

 Role                             Responsibility

 Shift Lead                       Verifies the line clearance.

 Operator                         Clears the line and loads the new materials.

 QA Technician                    Gives first-off approval.

 Batching Operator                Flushes the supply line from the mixing tank.


4.0 SAFETY AND MATERIALS
Personal protective equipment: Safety glasses; Safety shoes; Chemical-resistant gloves
Materials: Labels; Caps (28 mm); Flush water
Tools: Lint-free wipes

5.0 RELATED DOCUMENTS AND RECORDS
Documents: QP-306 First-Off Approval; WI-210 Filler Setup and Nozzle Check; WI-212 Labeler Setup
and Label Roll Change
Records: FRM-PR-05 Line Clearance Checklist

6.0 CLEAR THE LINE
 1. Operator: Run out the old product until the filler bowl is empty. Stop the line.
 2. Operator: Remove every bottle, cap and label of the old product from the line, the floor and the reject
    bins.
 3. Operator: Remove the label roll from the labeler and return part rolls to the label cage with the roll
    tag attached.
 4. Batching Operator: Flush the supply line from MIX-1 or MIX-2 to the filler with water for 10
    minutes. Drain.
 5. Shift Lead: Walk the line with FRM-PR-05 Line Clearance Checklist. Sign only when nothing from
     the old product remains.

7.0 LOAD THE NEW PRODUCT
 1. Operator: Load the new labels and set up the labeler as described in WI-212.
 2. Operator: Prime the filler and set up as described in WI-210.
 3. QA Technician: First-off approval as described in QP-306, including a label and lot code check.
Note: Target changeover time: 30 minutes.
