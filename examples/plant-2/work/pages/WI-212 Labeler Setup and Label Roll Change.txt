     Labeler Setup and Label Roll Change                                                                   Rev D


  Effective                    Owner                        Prepared by                  Approved by
  2025-09-08                   Production                   M. Alvarez                   D. Whitfield


PURPOSE

Apply labels straight, wrinkle-free and in the right place, and change rolls without a line stop.

SCOPE

LAB-1, LAB-2 and LAB-3 pressure-sensitive labelers.

ROLES

 ROLE                                   RESPONSIBILITY


 Operator                               Sets up the labeler and changes rolls.

 Line Lead                              Decides whether to stop the line for repeated jams.

 Maintenance Technician                 Adjusts sensors and replaces rollers.


PPE
     Safety glasses
     Safety shoes

TOOLS

     Splice tape
     Scissors
     Lint-free wipes
     Isopropyl alcohol

M AT E R I A L S

     Labels

PROCEDURE

  Setup

 STEP         WHO                       ACTION

 1            Operator                  Use only label rolls with a green QA release sticker (see QP-308). Rolls
                                        from a supplier not listed in QP-309 are not to be loaded.

 2            Operator                  Check the label matches the product and the artwork revision on the
                                        schedule.

 3            Operator                  Thread the web as shown on the diagram inside the labeler door.
 4            Operator                           Set web tension to mark 4 on the dancer arm. Set the gap sensor so it
                                                 reads the gap between labels, not the label edge.

 5            Operator                           Clean the peel plate and the drive roller with isopropyl alcohol at every
                                                 roll change.


  Roll change

 STEP         WHO                                ACTION


 6            Operator                           Prepare the new roll on the spare spindle before the old roll runs out.

 7            Operator                           Splice the new roll to the old one as described in WI-215.

 8            Operator                           Write the roll number, supplier and lot on FRM-PR-12 Label Roll Log.


  Jams and web breaks

 STEP         WHO                                ACTION

 9            Operator                           First jam: clear, re-thread, restart.

 10           Operator                           Three jams or web breaks in one hour: tell the Line Lead. Put the roll
                                                 aside with a hold tag as described in QP-315 and load a roll from a
                                                 different lot.

 11           Line Lead                          If jams continue on a second lot, call the Maintenance Technician and the
                                                 QA Technician.
