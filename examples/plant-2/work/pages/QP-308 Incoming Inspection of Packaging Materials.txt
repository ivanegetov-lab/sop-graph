 Purpose

Stop bad bottles, caps, labels and cases at the dock, before they reach a line.

 Scope

Every delivery of bottles, caps, labels and shipper cases.

 Roles

 Role                              Responsibility

 QA Technician                     Inspects and releases or rejects each lot.

 Receiving Clerk                   Holds the delivery in quarantine until release.

 Purchasing                        Is told of every rejection.


 PPE                                         Tools                                   Materials

     Safety shoes                             Calipers                                Bottles
     Cut-resistant gloves                     Check scale (0.1 g)                     Caps
                                              Go/no-go neck gauge                     Labels
                                              Barcode verifier                        Shipper cases

 Procedure

For every lot
 Step      Who                        Action

 1         Receiving Clerk            Place the delivery in the quarantine lane with a yellow QUARANTINE tag. See
                                      WH-601.

 2         QA Technician              Check the supplier is on the approved list in QP-309. If not, reject.

 3         QA Technician              Check the certificate of conformance is with the delivery and matches the lot
                                      number.

Checks by material
 Step      Who                        Action

 4         QA Technician              Bottles (sample of 13): weight, height, neck finish with the go/no-go gauge, no
                                      flash or contamination.
 5          QA Technician                    Caps (sample of 13): size, colour, liner present, fit on a sample bottle.

 6          QA Technician                    Labels (3 rolls per lot): artwork and revision against the binder, print quality,
                                             barcode grade C or better, roll width, core diameter 76 mm, labels wound
                                             outward.

 7          QA Technician                    Shipper cases (sample of 5): dimensions, board grade stamp, print.

Decision
 Step       Who                              Action

 8          QA Technician                    Pass: green RELEASED sticker on every pallet or roll. Record on FRM-QA-08
                                             Incoming Inspection Record.

 9          QA Technician                    Fail: red REJECTED tag, hold as described in QP-315, and email Purchasing
                                             the same day.
