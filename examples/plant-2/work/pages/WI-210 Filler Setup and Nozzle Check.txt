 Purpose

Set up a rotary filler so that every nozzle fills inside the weight limits.

 Scope

FIL-1, FIL-2 and FIL-3.

 Roles

 Role                                          Responsibility

 Operator                                      Sets up and adjusts the filler.

 Line Lead                                     Is told about any nozzle taken out of service.

 Maintenance Technician                        Repairs nozzles and valves.


 PPE                                       Tools                                      Materials

     Safety glasses                          Check scale (0.1 g)                       Sanitizer
     Chemical-resistant gloves               Nozzle wrench
     Safety shoes                            Sample tray


  CAUTION: Never reach into the filler carousel while it is turning. Use the jog button with the guard
  closed.


 Procedure

Setup

 Step       Who                       Action

 1          Operator                  Select the product recipe on the filler panel. Confirm the target weight on
                                      screen matches the table below.

 2          Operator                  Prime the bowl. Run two full rotations into the sample tray and discard.

 3          Operator                  Weigh one bottle from every nozzle (12 nozzles on FIL-1; all nozzles on FIL-2
                                      and FIL-3). Write each weight on FRM-PR-10 Filler Setup Sheet.

 4          Operator                  Any nozzle more than 3 g from the others: stop, tell the Line Lead, and call the
                                      Maintenance Technician.

Adjustment during the run
 Step       Who                       Action
 5          Operator                         If the hourly average drifts from target, you may adjust the fill setpoint by up to
                                             2 g up or down. Write the change and the time on FRM-PR-10.

 6          Operator                         If more than 2 g of adjustment is needed in one shift, stop adjusting and call
                                             the Maintenance Technician. Worn valve seals are the usual cause (see MP-
                                             501).

 7          Operator                         Dripping or stringing nozzle: wipe, run 10 bottles, check again. If it continues,
                                             take the nozzle out of service and tell the Line Lead.
