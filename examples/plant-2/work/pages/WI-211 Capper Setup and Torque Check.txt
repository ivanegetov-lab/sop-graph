     INSTRUCTION           Capper Setup and Torque Check                                       Rev C

 Purpose

Apply caps at the right torque so bottles neither leak nor are too hard to open.

 Scope

CAP-1, CAP-2 and CAP-3.

 Roles

 Role                                           Responsibility

 Operator                                       Sets up the capper and checks torque.

 Maintenance Technician                         Replaces chucks and adjusts clutches.


 PPE                                    Tools                                      Materials

     Safety glasses                       Torque tester                             Caps (28 mm)
     Safety shoes                         Chuck wrench                              Caps (38 mm)


  CAUTION: Cross-threaded caps mean the head height is wrong. Do not raise the torque to
  compensate.


 Procedure

Setup
 Step      Who                      Action

 1         Operator                 Fill the cap hopper. Check the cap chute is free and the caps are the right size
                                    and colour for the product.

 2         Operator                 Set the head height so the chuck sits square on the cap with the bottle on the
                                    plate.

 3         Operator                 Set the application torque from the table below.

Torque check

 Step      Who                      Action

 4         Operator                 At start-up and every 2 hours: take one bottle from each capping head.
                                    Measure removal torque with the torque tester.

 5         Operator                 Write the values on FRM-PR-11 Torque Check Sheet.
 6          Operator                        One head out of range: adjust that head and re-check. Two or more heads out
                                            of range: stop the line and call the Maintenance Technician.

 7          Operator                        Product capped since the last good check goes on hold as described in QP-
                                            315.

Application torque

 Cap               Used on                                                       Torque (in-lb)                Tolerance

 28 mm             Lines 1 and 3 (CAP-1, CAP-3)                                  12                            plus or minus 2

 38 mm             Line 2 (CAP-2), both bottle sizes                             17                            plus or minus 2
