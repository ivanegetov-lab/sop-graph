        PLANT 2                           Case Packer Vacuum Pump Inspection
   Maintenance Procedure
                             Document No.: MP-502               Revision: A        Effective: 2019-08-12

 Department: Maintenance     Prepared by: K. Petrov             Approved by: J. Brandt


1.0 PURPOSE
Find a failing vacuum pump before it stops the case packer.

2.0 SCOPE
The vacuum pumps on CPK-1, CPK-2 and CPK-3. Monthly task in the maintenance system.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

 Role                                      Responsibility

 Maintenance Technician                    Carries out the inspection.

 Maintenance Planner                       Orders spare parts and schedules repairs.


4.0 SAFETY AND MATERIALS
Personal protective equipment: Safety glasses; Hearing protection; Safety shoes
Materials: Vacuum pump inlet filter; Bearing VP-BRG-6205
Tools: Infrared thermometer; Vacuum gauge; Stethoscope

5.0 RELATED DOCUMENTS AND RECORDS
Documents: SP-402 Lockout/Tagout, Rev B; WI-213 Case Packer Setup and Glue Check
Records: Maintenance work order

6.0 INSPECTION (PUMP RUNNING)
 1. Maintenance Technician: Listen to the pump with the stethoscope at the drive end. Rumbling or
    grinding means a bearing is failing.
 2. Maintenance Technician: Measure the bearing housing temperature. More than 70 degrees C, or more
    than 15 degrees above last month, is a fail.
 3. Maintenance Technician: Read the vacuum at the pump inlet with the erector idle: at least 0.7 bar of
    vacuum.
 4. Maintenance Technician: Write the three readings on the work order every month, pass or fail.

7.0 SERVICE (PUMP LOCKED OUT)
 1. Maintenance Technician: Lock out the case packer using the group lock box, as described in SP-402,
    Rev B.
  2. Maintenance Technician: Replace the inlet filter if it is dark or the vacuum reading was low.
  3. Maintenance Technician: On a failed bearing check: replace bearing VP-BRG-6205 and check the
     coupling alignment.
  4. Maintenance Planner: Keep at least one bearing VP-BRG-6205 in stores.
