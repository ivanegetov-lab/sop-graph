     Lockout/Tagout                                                                                       Rev D


  Effective                    Owner                         Prepared by                Approved by
  2025-03-03                   Safety                        T. Greene                  D. Whitfield


PURPOSE

Make sure no machine can start or release stored energy while someone is working inside it.

SCOPE

All servicing, cleaning and jam clearing that puts any part of the body inside a guard, on any equipment at
Plant 2.

ROLES

 ROLE                    RESPONSIBILITY


 Authorized              Applies their own lock and tag. Operators and Maintenance Technicians who are trained
 Employee                and listed.

 Line Lead               Makes sure everyone affected knows the machine is locked out.

 Safety Manager          Audits this procedure every year.


PPE

     Safety glasses
     Safety shoes

TOOLS
     Personal padlock (one key)
     Lockout hasp
     Danger tag
     Valve lockout device
     Voltage tester

M AT E R I A L S
None.

PROCEDURE

  Applying

 STEP         WHO                       ACTION


 1            Authorized Employee       Tell the Line Lead and the operators which machine is being locked out.

 2            Authorized Employee       Stop the machine with its normal stop button.
 3            Authorized Employee                Switch off and lock the electrical disconnect. Close and lock the
                                                 compressed air isolation valve. Bleed the air.

 4            Authorized Employee                Case packers: also wait for the glue tank to cool below 60 degrees C or
                                                 keep the tank lid closed and tagged.

 5            Authorized Employee                One person, one lock, one key. Each person working on the machine
                                                 applies their own lock to the hasp.

 6            Authorized Employee                Try to start the machine. Confirm nothing moves. Test for voltage where
                                                 electrical work is planned.


  Removing

 STEP         WHO                                ACTION


 7            Authorized Employee                Tools out, guards back, everyone clear.

 8            Authorized Employee                Each person removes only their own lock. A lock left on at shift change
                                                 stays until its owner removes it or the Safety Manager authorises
                                                 removal.

 9            Authorized Employee                Tell the Line Lead the machine is ready to start.
