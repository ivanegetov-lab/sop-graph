 Purpose

Receive materials safely, check them against the order, and keep them in quarantine until Quality releases
them.

 Scope

All deliveries at dock doors 1 to 4.

 Roles

 Role                                      Responsibility

 Receiving Clerk                           Checks the delivery against the purchase order.

 Forklift Driver                           Unloads and puts away.

 Warehouse Supervisor                      Assigns dock doors and resolves shortages.

 QA Technician                             Releases packaging materials.


 PPE                                       Tools                                    Materials

     High-visibility vest                    Pallet jack                              Bottles
     Safety shoes                            Wheel chocks                             Caps
     Cut-resistant gloves                    Barcode scanner                          Labels
                                                                                      Shipper cases
                                                                                      Raw chemicals

 Procedure

Unloading
 Step       Who                        Action

 1          Warehouse Supervisor       Assign a dock door. Inbound packaging normally uses doors 3 and 4.

 2          Forklift Driver            Chock the trailer wheels and lock the dock leveler before entering.

 3          Forklift Driver            Drive at walking pace on the dock and in the receiving lanes: no faster than 3
                                       mph.

 4          Receiving Clerk            Check the delivery against the purchase order: item, quantity, lot numbers,
                                       damage. Note the time of arrival against the promised time.

Quarantine and put-away
 Step       Who                             Action

 5          Receiving Clerk                 Enter the delivery on FRM-WH-01 Receiving Log and in the inventory system.

 6          Receiving Clerk                 Packaging materials go to the quarantine lane with a yellow QUARANTINE
                                            tag until the QA Technician releases them (see QP-308).

 7          Forklift Driver                 After release, put away to the location on the tag. Oldest lot in front.

 8          Warehouse Supervisor            A delivery more than 2 hours late, or short, is reported to Purchasing and to
                                            the Production Planner the same shift.
