 Purpose

Erect, pack and seal shipper cases that survive the pallet.

 Scope

CPK-1, CPK-2 and CPK-3.

 Roles

 Role                                   Responsibility

 Operator                               Sets up the case packer.

 Maintenance Technician                 Services the glue system and the vacuum pump.


 PPE                                    Tools                                     Materials

     Safety glasses                       Infrared thermometer                     Shipper cases
     Heat-resistant gloves at the                                                  Hot-melt glue
     glue tank
     Safety shoes


  CAUTION: Hot-melt glue causes deep burns. Do not open the tank lid without heat-resistant
  gloves.


 Procedure

Setup

 Step      Who                      Action

 1         Operator                 Load the case magazine with the flaps facing the operator side. Do not mix
                                    case sizes.

 2         Operator                 Glue tank set to 175 degrees C. Wait for the ready light. Check the hose
                                    temperature with the infrared thermometer.

 3         Operator                 Check the vacuum gauge on the case erector reads at least 0.6 bar of
                                    vacuum with a case on the cups.

 4         Operator                 Run three cases. Check they are square and that all four flaps are glued.

During the run
 Step      Who                      Action
 5          Operator                        Every hour: tear open one sealed case. Fibre tear on both glue lines is a pass.
                                            Write it on FRM-PR-13 Case Seal Check.

 6          Operator                        Low vacuum or cases not erecting: check the cups for wear, then call the
                                            Maintenance Technician (see MP-502).

 7          Operator                        Never clear a jam with the machine running. For any work inside the guards,
                                            lock out as described in SP-402.
