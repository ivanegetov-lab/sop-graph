PURPOSE

Make sure nobody carries out a procedure they have not been trained and signed off on.

SCOPE

All production, quality, maintenance and warehouse roles at Plant 2.

ROLES

 ROLE                                RESPONSIBILITY


 Training Coordinator                Keeps the training matrix.

 Line Lead                           Signs off operators on the line.

 Department Manager                  Approves the list of required procedures for each role.


PPE
None.

TOOLS

None.

M AT E R I A L S

None.

PROCEDURE

  Rules

 STEP         WHO                       ACTION

 1            Training Coordinator      Keep FRM-TR-01 Training Matrix current: one row per person, one
                                        column per procedure.

 2            Line Lead                 A new operator works with a qualified operator until signed off on FRM-
                                        TR-02 Sign-Off Sheet for each procedure in the table below.

 3            Training Coordinator      When a procedure is revised, everyone trained on it is retrained within 30
                                        days. The document owner tells the Training Coordinator on the day of
                                        issue.

 4            Department Manager        Review the required procedures for each role every year.
