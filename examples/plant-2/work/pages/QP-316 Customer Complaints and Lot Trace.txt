        PLANT 2                               Customer Complaints and Lot Trace
     Quality Procedure
                              Document No.: QP-316               Revision: B        Effective: 2020-11-02

 Department: Quality          Prepared by: H. Costa              Approved by: J. Brandt


1.0 PURPOSE
Answer every customer complaint and find all product from the same lot.

2.0 SCOPE
All complaints about product made at Plant 2.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

 Role                       Responsibility

 Quality Manager            Owns each complaint until it is closed.

 QA Technician              Carries out the lot trace.

 Shift Lead                 Supplies the production and downtime records for the shift.


4.0 SAFETY AND MATERIALS
Personal protective equipment: None
Materials: None
Tools: None

5.0 RELATED DOCUMENTS AND RECORDS
Documents: QP-315 Nonconforming Product and Hold
Records: FRM-QA-20 Complaint Record

6.0 LOT CODES
 1. QA Technician: Every bottle carries a lot code in the form P2-L3-261104-A: plant, line, date as year-
    month-day, shift.
 2. QA Technician: The lot code identifies the line and shift. Use it to pull the production report, the fill
    weight chart, the label roll log and the downtime log for that shift.

7.0 HANDLING A COMPLAINT
 1. Quality Manager: Open FRM-QA-20 Complaint Record within one working day.
 2. QA Technician: Trace the lot. List the materials used: bottle, cap and label lots.
 3. QA Technician: Place any remaining stock from the same lot on hold as described in QP-315.
 4. Shift Lead: Give the QA Technician the shift's records and any notes within one working day.
 5. Quality Manager: Reply to the customer within 5 working days with the cause and the action taken.
